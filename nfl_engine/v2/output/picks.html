2026 regular season · weeks 8–18

Gridiron Engine

Stacked machine-learning ensemble predictions for every remaining game. The model never sees the Vegas line, so the edge column shows where it disagrees with the market.

Projected final records

Current record plus the sum of win probabilities for each remaining game.

How it backtests

Each period was predicted by a model trained only on earlier seasons. ATS needs 52.4% to break even at standard −110 odds.

Data and models

  • Results, rest days, home/neutral site, division games, roof, surface, weather, Vegas lines: nflverse schedules, 1999–2026
  • Team scoring, EPA per play, turnovers, sacks: nflverse weekly team stats, 2012–2026; EPA is also adjusted for opponent strength and split into passing and rushing matchups
  • Travel: miles traveled, time zones crossed and what time kickoff feels like on each team's body clock
  • Quarterbacks: each QB rated on EPA per play and completion % over expected, weighted toward recent games and pulled toward replacement level on small samples. Upcoming games use the depth-chart starter, swapped for the backup by injury status; ratings follow players when they change teams
  • Injuries: official NFL injury reports, each player weighted by position and his snap share with his current team; practice reports are used until final designations post
  • Elo: margin-of-victory adjusted ratings since 1999, regressed ⅓ each offseason
  • Win model: logistic regression, random forest, extra trees, gradient boosting, histogram GBM, neural net, SVM and k-NN, blended by a stacking meta-model, then averaged with the score model so the pick, win % and projected score always agree
  • Score model: separate stacked regressors for margin and total points

Later weeks use each team's current form, Elo, starting QB and season-average injury load, so they will shift as games are played. The model retrains weekly; injuries and depth charts refresh daily.
