2026 regular season · weeks 8–18

Gridiron Engine

Stacked machine-learning ensemble predictions for every remaining game. The model never sees the Vegas line, so the edge column shows where it disagrees with the market.

Projected final records

Current record plus the sum of win probabilities for each remaining game.

How it backtests

Each period was predicted by a model trained only on earlier seasons. ATS needs 52.4% to break even at standard −110 odds.

Data and models

  • Results, rest days, home/neutral site, division games, roof, surface, weather, Vegas lines: nflverse schedules, 1999–2026
  • Team scoring, EPA per play, turnovers, sacks: nflverse weekly team stats, 2012–2026
  • Injuries: official NFL injury reports, position-weighted (QB ×6); Week 8 uses practice reports until final designations post
  • Elo: margin-of-victory adjusted ratings since 1999, regressed ⅓ each offseason
  • Win model: logistic regression, random forest, extra trees, gradient boosting, histogram GBM, neural net, SVM and k-NN, blended by a stacking meta-model
  • Score model: separate stacked regressors for margin and total points

Weeks 9–18 use each team's current form, Elo and season-average injury load, so they will shift as games are played. Re-run the engine weekly.
