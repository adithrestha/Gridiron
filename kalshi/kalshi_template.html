Kalshi Edge Board

Read this first. Our game model is about as accurate as Vegas, not better: against the spread it went 51–53% in backtests, under the 52.4% needed to profit at standard odds. Kalshi's game prices track Vegas closely, so a big gap between the model and Kalshi usually means the model is missing news (an injury, a role change, a depth-chart move).

To account for that, every probability here is pulled most of the way back toward Kalshi's price before it's compared, and a market is only flagged when it still clears the price plus Kalshi's fee. The player-prop math is newer and hasn't been tested against real prop results. Treat flags as places to look closer, not bets the numbers have proven.
